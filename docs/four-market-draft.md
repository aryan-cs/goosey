# Four-market selection (draft)

Requested titles:

1. Will there be a white person on the winning team?
2. Which volunteer will win the arm wrestle challenge?
3. Will the MC do a 67?
4. Will a Waterloo team win?

These are a pending replacement selection, not active market definitions. Do not
seed them or replace existing markets until the following resolution details
are supplied:

- Which overall winning team(s) qualify, particularly if HTN announces multiple
  overall winners instead of ranking a single first place.
- For the first market, evidence must be voluntary self-identification; never
  infer an individual's race from their name, photograph or appearance. Missing
  evidence is not sufficient to resolve NO.
- Arm-wrestling competitor names, event start/cutoff and official result source.
  Existing contracts are binary. Represent this as a grouped event with one
  YES/NO contract per competitor; don't convert it to an arbitrary single YES/NO
  question. Tie/cancellation rules must also be specified.
- Whether “67” means saying “six seven”, doing the gesture, or either, and the
  ceremony/time window covered.
- Whether a Waterloo team requires one enrolled University of Waterloo student,
  a majority, or all members. Use confirmed enrollment, not assumed affiliation.

Existing badge holdings reference a pinned catalog. Introducing this selection
requires a separate catalog/save migration so old positions cannot be attached
to these new questions. Database-backed price history remains the intended
source of truth; do not synthesize a historical chart for new markets.
